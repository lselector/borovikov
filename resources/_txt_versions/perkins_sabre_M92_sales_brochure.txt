                            THE PERKINS SABRE M92 - Engine Specifications
                                             Engine Configuration: 4 cylinder, in-line vertical, 4 stroke,
                                             water cooled

                                             Aspiration: Natural

                                             Bore / Stroke: 103mm (4.1in) x 127mm (5.00in)

                                             Displacement: 4.23 litre (258 cu. in.)

                                             Compression Ratio: 18.5:1

                                             Combustion System: Fastram direct injection

                                             Engine Rotation: Clockwise viewed from front

                                             Fuel Injection Equipment: Lucas rotary with electric and
                                             manual stop.

                                             Governing: Mechanical – all speed.

                                             Continuous Engine Operating Angles: 20° front end up, 25°
                                             sideways (35° sideways intermittent)
